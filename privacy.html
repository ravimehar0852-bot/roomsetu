Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Information we collect

  • Account: name, email address, mobile number (optional for tenants) and your role (tenant or owner).
  • Owners: property details, photos, and a contact number provided for your listing.
  • Tenants: enquiries you send (name, mobile number, message) and rooms you save.

How we use it

To run your account, show approved listings, deliver your enquiries to the relevant property owner, review listings, and keep the platform safe.

Who can see what

  • An owner's contact number is not shown publicly. Admins can see it for review.
  • When you send an enquiry, the owner of that property and our administrators can read it.
  • Your saved rooms are visible only to you.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore and Storage). Your login session is kept in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can update your name and mobile number from your dashboard. To request deletion of your account or data, email .

Children

The platform is intended for users aged 18 and above, or minors with a parent or guardian's involvement.
