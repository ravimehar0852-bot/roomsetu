About Us

Kota Room Finder helps students, working professionals and families find rooms, PGs, hostels and rental properties in Kota, Rajasthan – close to coaching institutes and popular localities.

What we do

  • Property owners list their rooms and PGs for free.
  • Every listing is reviewed by our team before it appears publicly.
  • Students and tenants search, save favourites and send enquiries directly to owners.

How it works

Search for a room, compare properties, contact the owner, then arrange a visit and finalize the rental agreement directly with the owner.

Please note

Kota Room Finder is a listing platform. We are not a party to any rental agreement. Always visit the property and verify details and documents before paying any money.
